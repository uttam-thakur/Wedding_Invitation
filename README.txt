ROYAL WEDDING INVITATION

Files:
- index.html   Main invitation markup
- styles.css   All styling and responsive layouts
- script.js    Door animation, browser-generated celebration music, countdown,
               scratch-to-reveal date card, scroll reveals and WhatsApp RSVP

How to use:
1. Keep all three files in the same folder.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser, or upload the folder to your hosting.
3. Click "Enter the palace" once. The doors open and the original instrumental
   melody starts from that user interaction (browser autoplay rules apply).
4. Scratch the blue card on the hero section with a mouse or finger to reveal
   the wedding date.

The music is synthesized in the browser with the Web Audio API, so no external
music download or third-party audio URL is required.
